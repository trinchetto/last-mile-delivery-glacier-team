## 🎯 PRODUCTION NOTES

### Slides to Prepare:
1. **Opening**: "19.2% Late Deliveries = 14,000+ Unhappy Customers"
2. **Solution**: DeliveryIQ logo + tagline
3. **Business Impact**: Bullet points with icons
4. **Closing**: Team credits + GitHub link

### Demo Checklist:
- [ ] Prepare 3 pre-loaded examples (low risk, high risk, carrier comparison)
- [ ] Ensure UI is responsive and polished
- [ ] Have backup screenshots in case of technical issues
- [ ] Test chat interface with 1-2 pre-scripted queries
- [ ] Record screen at 1080p resolution

### Team Coordination:
- **Team Member 1**: Opening hook + Demo Example 1 voiceover
- **Team Member 2**: Solution intro + Chat interface demo voiceover
- **Team Member 3**: Risk analysis + Business impact + Closing
- **All members**: Group intro and closing

### Timing Breakdown:
- Opening: 20 sec
- Solution: 25 sec
- Demo: 75 sec (most important!)
- Business Impact: 35 sec
- Closing: 25 sec
- **Total**: 180 seconds (3 minutes)

### Tips for Recording:
1. Speak slowly and clearly—enthusiasm is good, but clarity is essential
2. Use transitions between speakers to keep energy high
3. Practice the demo multiple times to ensure smooth flow
4. Record demo video separately, then add voiceover for better quality
5. Use background music (subtle, non-distracting) during demo sections
6. Add captions/subtitles for accessibility

### What to Emphasize:
- **Data-driven**: You analyzed 73K real shipments
- **Proactive vs Reactive**: Predict problems before they happen
- **Visual impact**: Traffic light system (🟢🟡🔴) is intuitive
- **Real business problem**: 19.2% late rate is compelling
- **Achievable solution**: Built in 48 hours, ready to scale
